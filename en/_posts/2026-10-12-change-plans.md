Trips often don't go as planned. The line is too long, the weather turns bad, or you change your mind. FlexTrip aims to be "a trip plan you can change flexibly," so when you edit your itinerary, your schedule is recalculated right away.

## Reordering visits

Open the ⋮ menu of the visit you want to move, and you'll find **Move up** (위로 이동), **Move down** (아래로 이동), and **Set position** (위치 지정). You can also press and hold the ⠿ handle on the left of a visit row and drag it.

When you change the order, all arrival and departure times are recalculated. Travel options are tied to a **pair of places**, so when a new combination appears (for example, Eiffel Tower → Arc de Triomphe), that leg shows **Add travel option** (이동수단 추가). If you've added options for the same pair before, you can reuse them as is.

**Set position** lets you pick directly where a visit falls in that day's order. Places you haven't decided when to visit can be kept in **Spare slots** (예비 슬롯) and added to your itinerary later.

## Times you can't miss: fixed arrival time

Some visits require you to arrive at a specific time, like a booked observation deck entry or the start of a show. Set a **Fixed arrival time** (고정 도착 시각) in **Edit visit** (방문 편집) from the visit menu.

On the same screen, you can also change the **Stay time** (체류 시간) and **Extra costs** (추가 비용) such as admission fees.

## Warnings tell you when you're running late

If earlier plans run long and you can't make a fixed time, that visit gets a **Late** (지연) label, and a **Warnings** (경고) chip appears at the top of the sheet.

Tap the Warnings chip to open the warning list, and tap an item to jump straight to the day and visit causing it. Try shortening the stay at an earlier visit, switching to a faster travel mode (for example, a taxi instead of walking), or reordering visits, and see whether the warning goes away. Arriving early isn't a warning; it's shown as "Wait" (대기).

## Made a mistake? Undo

Right after you delete or move a visit, a notification with an **Undo** (실행 취소) button briefly appears at the bottom of the screen. Even after it disappears, you can use the ↶ (Undo) and ↷ (Redo, 다시 실행) buttons at the top of the sheet to step back one change at a time.

{% include shot.html src="06-undo" alt="Undo notification: Deleted the Arc de Triomphe visit." caption="Undo" %}

Even actions that change many things at once, like adding lodging and flights, are fully reversed with a single undo.

## Summary

- Reorder visits and change their days with the menu or by dragging, and your schedule is recalculated right away.
- Enter times you can't miss as fixed arrival times, and check warnings if you're running late.
- Undo your mistakes.
