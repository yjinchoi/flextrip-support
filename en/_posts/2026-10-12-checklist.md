Before you leave, there's a mix of things to pack and things to do. Some are items like your passport and charger; others are tasks like booking a museum time slot or exchanging money. FlexTrip's **Checklist** (체크리스트) keeps both in one list, organized by category.

## The Checklist tab

Tap the **Checklist** tab on the trip itinerary screen to see that trip's list.

{% include shot.html src="04-checklist" alt="Checklist: 2/7 done, with To-dos & bookings, Documents & payment, and Electronics categories" caption="Checklist tab" %}

- Your progress is shown at the top, such as "2/7 done" (2/7 완료).
- Items are grouped by **category** (분류), such as To-dos & bookings (할 일·예약), Documents & payment (서류·결제), and Electronics (전자기기), and each category shows how many are done.
- Check off an item to mark it done, and its name gets crossed out. Use the eye icon to hide completed items or show them again.

## Adding items and categories

Type a name in the input box at the top and tap **+ Add** (+ 추가). Choose a category with the **Category: …** (분류: …) button below the input box.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="04-checklist-add" alt="Category picker sheet: 10 categories including To-dos & bookings, Documents & payment, Bags & storage, Electronics, and Connectivity" caption="Choosing a category" %}
{% include shot.html src="04-checklist-suggest" alt="Suggestion when typing power bank: Electronics › Power bank · Apply" caption="Category suggestion" %}
</div>

For common items, the app suggests a category. For example, type "power bank" (보조배터리), and "Suggested: Electronics › Power bank" appears. Tap **Apply** (적용) to set the category and the specific item in one go.

You can change category names, order, and icons in **Settings > Checklist categories** (설정 > 체크리스트 분류). You can also save lists you use often as templates and load them for your next trip (**Settings > Checklist templates**, 설정 > 체크리스트 템플릿).

## Open places to buy, right from the list

Items you need to buy before your trip, such as an eSIM or a universal adapter, have a shopping cart icon. Tap it to see where you can buy that item.

{% include shot.html src="04-checklist-shop" alt="Where to buy sheet: eSIM · France, Coupang, Airalo, Yesim" caption="Where to buy an eSIM" %}

For an eSIM, the app figures out your destination country from your itinerary and opens the product page for that country directly. In this example, it's "eSIM · France." Once you've bought something, check it off and the where-to-buy button goes away.

<p class="tip">Some of the where-to-buy links are affiliate links. There's no extra cost to you, and prices are the same. If you'd rather not see them, turn off "Show travel product suggestions" (여행 상품 추천 보기) in Settings, and the where-to-buy buttons go away.</p>

## Summary

- Manage things to pack and things to do in one list, organized by category.
- Type a name and the app suggests a category.
- For things you need to buy, you can open places to buy them right away.
