Most trips start with booking flights and lodging. You need to know when your flight lands and which neighborhood you're staying in before you can plan the rest. In FlexTrip, you can look for flights and lodging on the trip's **Info** (정보) tab and add what you've booked straight to your itinerary.

## "Lodging & flights" on the Info tab

Tap the **Info** tab on the trip itinerary screen, and you'll find the **Lodging & flights** (숙소·항공편) section at the very top. If nothing's there yet, you'll see a short note and two buttons:

- **Find & add lodging** (숙소 찾기·등록)
- **Find & add flights** (항공편 찾기·등록)

{% include shot.html src="02-info-bookings-empty" alt="Lodging & flights section on the Info tab with Find & add lodging and Find & add flights buttons" caption="Info tab — Lodging & flights" %}

## Adding a flight

Tap **Find & add flights** and enter the following.

1. **Departure airport and arrival airport** (출발 공항·도착 공항): Pick them in the place search box. The **Swap departure and arrival** (출발·도착 바꾸기) button in the middle makes it easy to set up your return flight.
2. **Departure date and time** and **arrival date and time** (출발 날짜·시각, 도착 날짜·시각): Enter both in **that airport's local time**. Just copy the times printed on your ticket.
3. **Flight number** (편명, for example, KE901), **cost per person** (1인 비용), and **notes** (메모): Optional.

If you haven't bought your tickets yet, tap **Find flights** (항공권 찾기). It opens flight search sites (Trip.com, Aviasales, Google Flights) in your browser with the airports and dates you entered.

### Check the preview, then add

When you tap **Add to itinerary** (일정에 등록), the app first shows you how the flight will fit into your itinerary.

{% include shot.html src="02-flight-preview" alt="Flight preview: Incheon International Airport 13:00 → Charles de Gaulle Airport 18:30, 12 h 30 min" caption="Flight preview" %}

- Flight time is calculated automatically from the two airports' time zones (12 hours 30 minutes in this example).
- The departure airport is set up in your itinerary so that you **leave at the departure time**. If the airport is already your trip's departure place, that visit is used as is. If you need to go to the airport in the middle of other plans, a fixed time is set so you arrive at the airport 2 hours before departure.
- The arrival airport is added as a new visit with its **arrival time fixed**. The rest of your plans are calculated from that time onward.

Tap **Add** (등록) and you're done. If you made a mistake, tap **Undo** (실행 취소) in the notification at the bottom of the screen to reverse it all at once.

## Adding lodging

In **Find & add lodging**, pick your lodging, and the check-in and check-out dates are pre-filled with your trip dates, along with the number of nights, such as "4 nights" (4박). It's handy to enter the total you paid in **Total lodging cost** (숙박비 전체) and details like your booking number in the notes.

Tap **Find lodging** (숙소 찾기) to open lodging booking sites near your lodging's location, with the same dates and number of travelers. Once you've booked, tap **Add to itinerary**.

{% include shot.html src="02-lodging-preview" alt="Lodging preview: Marais Hotel, 4 nights, where it's added on each day" caption="Lodging preview" %}

Your lodging is added as **the last visit of the day for every night you stay**, so the next day's plans automatically start from your lodging. In the preview, you can check where it goes on each day, such as "Add after Charles de Gaulle Airport" or "No visits · Add." If that lodging is already the last stop of the day, it isn't added again.

## After you add them

The section on the Info tab neatly lists the lodging and flights you added. Tap the lodging row to go to the lodging's place details.

{% include shot.html src="02-info-bookings-done" alt="Info tab: Marais Hotel 5/12 → 5/16, 4 nights; Incheon International Airport → Charles de Gaulle Airport 5/12 13:00 KE901" caption="Added lodging and flights" %}

Now go back to the **Itinerary** (일정) tab, and Day 1 is filled in as "Incheon International Airport → (flight) → Charles de Gaulle Airport → lodging," and from Day 2 on, each day starts at your lodging. All that's left is adding the places you want to go to each day.

<p class="tip">Some links in the flight and lodging search lists are affiliate links. There's no extra cost to you: when you book through one of these links, the site pays the app maker a small commission. Prices are the same. To open plain links instead, turn off "Show travel product suggestions" (여행 상품 추천 보기) in Settings.</p>
