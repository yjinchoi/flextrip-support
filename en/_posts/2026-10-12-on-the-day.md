You make plans so you can use them on the road. FlexTrip doesn't replace your map or taxi apps — it **opens the apps you already use, right from your itinerary**. No need to search for your destination again.

## Directions: in the map app you want

Tap the arrow (Directions) on the right of a leg row in your itinerary, and the **Choose a directions app** (길찾기 앱 선택) sheet opens.

{% include shot.html src="05-directions-chooser" alt="Choose a directions app: Marais Hotel → Louvre Museum by transit, Google Maps, Apple Maps, with Korea-only apps grayed out" caption="Choose a directions app" %}

- Your map app opens with the starting point, destination, and selected travel mode (transit, walking, or driving) already filled in.
- You can only choose apps installed on your phone. Naver Map, KakaoMap, and TMAP support routes in Korea only, so they're grayed out for legs abroad.
- Under **Find routes & travel times on the web** (웹에서 경로·소요 시간 찾기) at the bottom, you can compare ways to travel between cities and their fares with Google Maps transit (web) or Rome2Rio.

When you just want to get to a single visit, use the Directions button on the place details or the map marker card. It starts from your current location.

## Calling a taxi

If a leg's travel mode is **Taxi** (택시), the taxi icon in your itinerary is itself a button.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="05-taxi-leg" alt="Day 3 taxi leg: taxi 25 min €25.00, to Marais Hotel" caption="Taxi leg" %}
{% include shot.html src="05-taxi-sheet" alt="Taxi app sheet: to Marais Hotel · France, Uber, Bolt, FREENOW" caption="Taxi apps" %}
</div>

Tap it to see a list of taxi apps popular in that country (for France: Uber, Bolt, FREENOW). Apps that can take a destination, like Uber, open with the destination already filled in. If you don't have the app, you're taken to the app store. The suggested apps for each country are for reference and may vary by city.

## Finding tickets and tours

Tap **Find tickets & tours** (입장권·투어 찾기) in a visit row's ⋮ menu to see where you can buy tickets for that place.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="05-visit-menu" alt="Louvre Museum visit menu: Edit visit, Find tickets & tours, Move down, Set position, Delete visit" caption="Visit menu" %}
{% include shot.html src="05-ticket-sheet" alt="Find tickets & tours: Louvre Museum, Google Search, MyRealTrip, Klook, KKday, GetYourGuide" caption="Find tickets & tours" %}
</div>

It opens a Google search and travel product sites (MyRealTrip, Klook, KKday, GetYourGuide, and more) in your browser using the place's name. If the place has an official website on file, the official site appears at the top. After buying tickets, come back to the app and enter the amount as an admission fee, and it'll be included in your cost totals.

<p class="tip">Some links in the tickets and tours list are affiliate links. There's no extra cost to you, and prices are the same.</p>

## Summary

- Directions open in your installed map app with the start, destination, and travel mode filled in.
- For taxi legs, tap the icon to open taxi apps for that country.
- Find tickets and tours right from the visit menu.
