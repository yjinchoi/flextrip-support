You can't know the weather at your destination before you leave. If a day is mostly outdoors, it helps to decide in advance where to go if it rains. In Flex Trip Planner, you can prepare a backup plan in a **spare slot** and, after checking the weather that morning, replace that day's plan in one step.

Our example is a 3-day trip to Jeju Island. Day 2 is Seongsan Ilchulbong and Udo Island, and Day 3 is the Yeongsil trail on Hallasan — plans that depend on the weather.

The app's screens are in Korean. Below, button names are given in Korean with an English translation.

## 1. Plan day by day

When you create the trip, choose **일별 일정** (Daily schedule) as the plan type, and each day gets its own card. Add visits to each day.

## 2. Make a rainy-day plan in a spare slot

At the bottom of the schedule tab, tap **예비 슬롯 추가** (Add spare slot) to get an empty card named like "예비 1" (Spare 1). Use **이름 변경** (Rename) in the card's menu to give it a name that says when it's for, such as "2일차 비 오는 날" (Day 2, rainy day), and add indoor visits.

{% include shot.html src="p3-spares" alt="Spare slot 'Day 2 rainy day' opened: Aqua Planet Jeju 09:00–11:00, Jeju Folk Village 11:00–13:00, with 'Day 3 rainy day' below" caption="A rainy-day plan per day, kept in spare slots" %}

- Spare slots have no date. Use the name to show which day each one is for.
- Spare slot costs are not counted in the trip total.

## 3. On the morning, replace the day with the spare slot

If it's raining on the morning of Day 2, open the menu of the "2일차 비 오는 날" card and tap **다른 슬롯과 병합…** (Merge with another slot…).

{% include shot.html src="p3-merge-menu" alt="Menu for Day 2 rainy day: rename, duplicate, merge with another slot, move down, make a full route trip from this slot, delete slot" caption="Spare slot menu" %}

Choose **2일차** (Day 2) as the day to change, and you're asked to pick **병합** (Merge) or **대체** (Replace). To swap the whole day, choose **대체** (Replace).

{% include shot.html src="p3-choice" alt="'Day 2 rainy day → Day 2' choice: merge (combine both plans) or replace (swap Day 2's plan and keep the original in a spare slot)" caption="Choosing merge or replace" %}

A confirmation screen shows exactly what will change.

{% include shot.html src="p3-replace" alt="Replace Day 2: 2 places from Day 2 rainy day move to Day 2, the current 2 places on Day 2 are kept in spare slot 'Day 2 before', departure and arrival places stay, and the spare slot goes to the trash" caption="Checking before you replace" %}

- The spare slot's visits move to Day 2.
- The original Day 2 plan isn't lost. It's kept in a spare slot named **"2일차 이전"** (Day 2 before), so you can switch back the same way if the weather clears.
- The trip's departure and arrival places stay where they are.
- The used spare slot goes to the trash.

Tap **대체** (Replace), and Day 2 becomes the indoor plan. To undo right away, tap **실행 취소** (Undo) in the toolbar.

{% include shot.html src="p3-done" alt="Day 2 card after replacing: starts from the previous day's last place, Hamdeok Beach, then Aqua Planet Jeju and Jeju Folk Village" caption="The schedule after replacing" %}

## Summary

- For days that are mostly outdoors, prepare a rainy-day plan in a **spare slot** and name it after the day.
- On the day, go to the spare slot's menu → **다른 슬롯과 병합…** (Merge with another slot…) → pick the day → **대체** (Replace) to swap the whole day.
- The original plan stays in a "Day N before" spare slot, so you can always switch back.
