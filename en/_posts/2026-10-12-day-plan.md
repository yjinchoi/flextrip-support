With your lodging and flights in place, it's time to fill in each day. A FlexTrip itinerary is made up of **visits** (방문, the places you go) and **legs** (구간, the travel between visits). It strings together time spent at each place and travel time to calculate your daily schedule automatically.

## Reading a day card

Tap a day card on the **Itinerary** (일정) tab to expand it. Here's Day 2 of the Paris trip:

{% include shot.html src="03-day-card" alt="Day 2 card: starts at Marais Hotel, metro 15 min, Louvre Museum 09:15–12:15, walk, Café de Flore, Notre-Dame Cathedral" caption="Day 2 card" %}

- Summary at the top: number of visits, start time, and the day's total cost (in the local currency and in KRW).
- **Start** (시작): The day starts from the previous night's lodging (the last place of the day before).
- Numbered rows are visits. Calculated arrival and departure times are shown, such as "09:15–12:15 · Stay 3 h" (체류 3시간).
- Rows between visits are legs. They show how you're getting there, such as metro or walking, along with the time and fare.

There are several ways to add a visit: tap a place on the map, choose one from My Places on the **Places** (장소) tab, or find a new one with place search and add it to that day.

## See the day's route on the map

Tap a card, and the map moves to that day's places, drawing numbered pins and the route in visit order. Pull the sheet down to see a bigger map. Each leg is shown with an icon, such as walking or metro, so you can check your route at a glance.

{% include shot.html src="03-day-map" alt="Numbered pins 1–4 and the route on a map of Paris, with an icon for how each leg is traveled" caption="The day's route" %}

## Choose how to travel each leg

Tap **Change** (변경) on a leg row to open the travel options sheet for that leg.

{% include shot.html src="03-leg-options" alt="Travel options for Marais Hotel → Louvre Museum: metro 15 min €2.50 selected, taxi 12 min €18.00, none" caption="Choosing how to travel" %}

- Add **several options** for one leg and choose the one you'll actually use. For example, add both the metro (15 min, €2.50) and a taxi (12 min, €18.00), then switch depending on the situation.
- Sort options **by order added, cost, or time** (등록순·비용순·시간순) to compare them.
- Tap **+ Add option** (+ 옵션 추가) to add a new option. You can enter the mode (metro, bus, train, taxi, walking, and more), travel time, fare, and line number.
- Tap **Add stop** (경유지 추가) to add a quick stop along the way.
- Tap **Directions** (길찾기) to check the actual route in a map app installed on your phone.

Options are shared between **the same pair of places**. A metro option you added for "lodging → Louvre" can be picked again for the same leg on another day, which saves you typing on trips where similar routes come up again and again.

## Check your costs

You can add **extra costs** (추가 비용) such as admission fees to visits (per person or in total), and fares to legs. Tap the **Costs** (비용) button on the **Costs** tab to see a breakdown of costs for the whole trip.

{% include shot.html src="03-cost-tab" alt="Costs screen: per-person and total cost for the Paris trip, with a cost-by-day table" caption="Costs screen" %}

- At the top, you'll see the cost per person and the total cost for all travelers.
- The **Cost by day** (일자별 비용) table shows which days cost the most.
- If you've entered an exchange rate for the trip currency, euro amounts are converted to KRW.

## Summary

- A day's plan = visits + legs. Your schedule is calculated automatically from time spent at places and travel time.
- Keep several travel options for each leg and choose the one that fits.
- Enter admission fees and fares to see costs by day and for the whole trip at a glance.
