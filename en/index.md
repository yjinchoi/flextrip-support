FlexTrip is a travel planning app for iOS and Android. Collect the places you want to visit, plan each day, and keep track of how you get between places and what it costs. When plans change, moving things around is easy.

<ul class="cards">
  <li><strong>Collect places</strong>Search the map or drop a pin to save places you want to visit.</li>
  <li><strong>Day-by-day plans</strong>Split your trip into slots and reorder visits.</li>
  <li><strong>Routes and directions</strong>Choose how you travel between stops and open directions in the map apps you already have.</li>
  <li><strong>Costs and currencies</strong>Record costs in any currency and see the totals.</li>
  <li><strong>Import and export</strong>Exchange places and trips as KML, KMZ, or CSV files.</li>
  <li><strong>Back up to your cloud</strong>Back up to your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud account.</li>
</ul>

## Your data stays on your device
FlexTrip has no sign-up. Your trip data is stored on your device, and backups go only to the cloud account you connect. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/en/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details.
